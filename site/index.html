Reading the colours

Ranges match Capa's tuning card. Trend = latest reading vs ~60 min earlier. On the direction charts, veer = clockwise = right shift (green, above the window mean) and back = anticlockwise = left shift (amber, below it). Readings older than the station's normal cadence are greyed as stale; missing stations show n/a. Nothing on this page is estimated or filled in.
