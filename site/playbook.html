AROME is the model to lead with
Best overall at the buoy: +0.2 kt bias, 2.5 kt average error, 19° direction error. Most other models forecast too little wind, often by 2–4 kt; the AI models (ECMWF AIFS, GraphCast) are worst at 3.4–4.5 kt too light.
Correct AROME by regime
NE–E: add about 2 kt and expect the wind about 15° left of forecast. Synoptic south: −2 kt. W–WNW: −1 kt. Sea breeze: +0.7 kt. Northerly: no correction.
NE–E goes right (most reliable trend)
About +10° between 11 and 14 h; 58 % of days veered; buoy and airport agree. Under about 12 kt it can swing 45° or more toward SE–S. Favour the right early.
Northerlies oscillate the most
Swings of about 25° on a roughly one-hour cycle: play the shifts. Lighter northerlies (under about 12 kt) lean left in the afternoon: 73 % backed, median −18°. Stronger ones show no lean.
The winter sea breeze is weak and late
A lull around 11:00, then it fills from about 215° and only reaches about 8.5 kt by 14:00. Timing the fill matters more than the shift.
Genoa choice
V-6M up to 15 kt, V-6H above. In North's 14–18 kt overlap, decide on the forecast trend and sea state.
Portals is probably lighter than the buoy
AROME estimates Portals at about 0.74× the buoy wind (0.63× in NW–N). That is a model estimate, not a measurement. Rule of thumb until on-board logs say otherwise: Portals ≈ buoy × 0.75, or ≈ airport × 0.8.

3Regime quick-reference cards

Frequencies are the share of winter race days at the buoy (270 classified days) and at the airport (1,966 days, 2010–26). Shift numbers are the change in mean wind direction from 11 h to 15 h at the buoy, Oct–Mar: + = veer (wind goes right), − = back (wind goes left). Tactics are inference from the statistics, not measured at Portals.

NE–E

Gregal / Llevant
15.9 % of buoy winter days · 17.2 % LEPA
Recognise it
NE 050–060° in the morning at 10–11 kt. The wettest regime.
Typical behaviour
Veers steadily from 054° (10 h) to 077° (16 h) at the buoy, holding 10.7–11.5 kt. Shift range 19.2°, cycle about 48 min. The NE wind at 065° gave the 100° shift and abandoned race at the Gold Cup 2026.
Shift tendency
↻ Goes RIGHT (veers) High: buoy and 16 years of LEPA agree
Median +10° from 11 h to 15 h, 58 % of days veered (55 days). Most of it happens 11–14 h, flat after. Under 12.4 kt: median +49°, 70 % veered, and on 17 of 27 days it swung more than 45°, mostly clockwise toward SE–S.
AROME correction
+2 kt, and rotate about 15° anticlockwise (AROME HD is −2.1 kt too light, MAE 2.9 kt, direction error 33°). Every model forecasts this regime too light.
Likely genoa
Mostly 10–15 kt: V-6M. Switch to V-6H if AROME shows 14 kt or more (corrected 16 kt or more).
On the water inference
Favour the right side early in the first race; start where you can tack onto port and go right. Don't get stuck left, especially under about 12 kt. Watch for a second breeze (Xaloc or Llevant) competing, and don't commit to a corner. After 14 h, play the shifts.

Northerly

Tramuntana / Mestral (NW–N–NNE)
20.7 % of buoy winter days · 22.1 % LEPA
Recognise it
A 330–355° breeze already at 10–11 kt at 08–10 h, with cloud over the Serra.
Typical behaviour
Backs from 354° (10 h) to 324° (16 h) and builds slightly to 12.6 kt at 14 h at the buoy. Biggest swings of any regime: 25.5° (10–90 %), cycle about 58 min, so roughly one full cycle per beat. Offshore wind in the lee means puffs and holes.
Shift tendency
⇄ Oscillating Moderate: LEPA does not confirm the backing
Overall median −7° (53 % backed, 53 days). Lighter northerlies (<12.2 kt at the buoy): median −18°, 73 % backed, mostly 14–16 h. Stronger northerlies: no lean (+1°).
AROME correction
None (bias −0.1 kt), but expect ±2.7 kt (MAE). AROME suggests Portals gets only about 0.63× the buoy wind here (model estimate).
Likely genoa
Often 6–10 or 10–15 kt at Portals. Choose for the lulls: usually V-6M.
On the water inference
Play the shifts before the sides. On lighter days, especially 14–16 h, lean left. Keep your head out of the boat for pressure lanes coming off the shore.

Sea breeze

Embat (S–SW)
24.4 % of buoy winter days · 18.9 % LEPA
Recognise it
Light N–NE land breeze at 08–10 h (about 4–5 kt), a calm patch around 11 h, then about 215° filling in from 12 h.
Typical behaviour
Only 8.5 kt at the buoy by 14 h (lighter than the 15–20 kt of summer). Settles at 215–218°. Swings 16.7°, cycle about 50 min. Gust factor 1.12.
Shift tendency
↻ Goes RIGHT (veers) Low to moderate: small trend, LEPA does not confirm
After the fill (13→16 h, 109 days): median +4°; veer 45 % / back 30 % / steady 25 %. Above 7.4 kt it veers more consistently (median +8°, only 17 % backed).
AROME correction
+0.7 kt (AROME HD bias −0.7 kt, MAE 2.1 kt). AROME France 2.5 km scores slightly better (MAE 1.9 kt).
Likely genoa
Usually 6–10 kt, sometimes 10–15 kt: V-6M.
On the water inference
An 11:00 warning may fall in the changeover: expect postponements, or a first beat in the dying northerly. Once in, a slight preference for the right when above about 7 kt. Timing the fill matters more than the trend.

W–WNW

Ponent
21.5 % of buoy winter days · 14.4 % LEPA
Recognise it
265–285° at about 12 kt from the morning onwards.
Typical behaviour
Fairly stable direction, building to 14.6 kt at 14–15 h at the buoy. Swings 17.6°, cycle about 53 min. AROME puts Portals at about 0.70× the buoy (model estimate).
Shift tendency
⇄ Oscillating Buoy and LEPA agree there is at most a weak lean right
No reliable trend: median +1°, veer 44 % / back 35 % / steady 21 % (57 days). Drifts slightly left to 13 h, back right after. Days at 14.8 kt or more lean slightly right (median +5°).
AROME correction
−1 kt (AROME HD forecasts it 0.9 kt too strong, MAE 2.3 kt).
Likely genoa
10–15 kt, sometimes 15–20 kt: V-6M or V-6H, decided in North's 14–18 kt overlap.
On the water inference
Standard shift-playing on the roughly 53-minute cycle. From the west the wind runs along the coast at Portals, so watch for shoreline effects on one side of the course.

Synoptic S–SE–SW

Xaloc / Migjorn
7.0 % of buoy winter days · 11.5 % LEPA
Recognise it
S–SW already blowing at about 11 kt at 10 h, with no morning land breeze first (unlike the sea breeze).
Typical behaviour
About 222°, 12.7–14.2 kt. The steadiest regime: swings 13.6°, cycle about 48 min. Gust factor 1.11.
Shift tendency
⇄ Oscillating Low: LEPA does not confirm
Buoy median +11° (61 % veered, 33 days), but the 17-year LEPA record shows 0°, and only 17 buoy days fall in Nov–Feb.
AROME correction
−2 kt (AROME HD +2.0 kt too strong), but the direction is very good (8° error).
Likely genoa
10–15 kt: V-6M. V-6H if gusts of 15 kt or more persist.
On the water inference
Little oscillation, so go for pressure and current. Lean right only if you see it veering before the start.

10.4 % of buoy winter days · 15.9 % LEPA
Recognise it
A NE drainage breeze off the land that dies around 11–12 h.
Typical behaviour
NE drainage dies about 12 h, then a weak SW from about 13 h. A full change of wind, not a shift. Direction swings 37.2° (23 days).
Shift tendency
○ New wind, not a shift Low: under 20 days

4Shift tendencies

Going upwind, a veer (wind going right) pays the boat to the right of the fleet, and a back pays the left. A persistent shift means get to the side it is shifting toward early. An oscillating breeze means tack on the headers and stay near the middle.

Does wind strength change the trend?

Each regime split at its median 11–16 h buoy speed.

  • NE–E: under 12.4 kt, median +49° and 70 % veered (27 days); 17 of those 27 days turned more than 45°, 14 of them clockwise, mostly ending E–SE to S–SW by 15 h. At 12.4 kt or more: median +4°, 46 % veered (28 days). The airport agrees: under 10.2 kt +15° with 58 % veered, against 0° and 31 % above it.
  • Northerly: under 12.2 kt, median −18° and 73 % backed (26 days). At 12.2 kt or more: +1°, 33 % backed (27 days). The airport shows no backing in either half.
  • Sea breeze: above 7.4 kt, median +8° and only 17 % backed. Below 7.4 kt, +5° with 45 % backing.
  • W–WNW: at 14.8 kt or more, median +5° (48 / 31 % veer / back). Below that, −1° (39 / 39 %).
  • Synoptic S: about 60 % veered at both strengths (median +14° under 13.4 kt, +6° above).

Buoy vs airport cross-check

On the same days the two stations put the trend in the same veer / back / steady category on 60–75 % of days, and opposite on only 0–15 % (NE–E: 71 % same, 48 days; Northerly: 70 % same, 15 % opposite, 40 days). They agree that NE–E veers, most on lighter days, and that W–WNW has at most a weak lean right. They disagree on the northerly backing, the synoptic-south veer and the sea-breeze veer. Where they disagree, trust the buoy for the bay, but treat those trends as moderate confidence.

West vs east side of the course: this cannot be determined from the data. The buoy is about 12 km ESE of Portals and the airport is inland, so neither spans the race course. On-board logs from both ends of the line, or a second boat or RIB with a logger, are the way to measure it.

Shifts and gusts

A typical day swings 18° (10–90 % range, standard deviation 8°) with an oscillation period of about 52 min (214 days). Gust factor (gust ÷ mean, mean of 5 kt or more) is 1.13 overall, only 1.11–1.15 across regimes: the open-water buoy sees steady breeze. Gusts off the mountains near Portals are probably stronger, but that is unmeasured.

7Buoy, airport & Portals

  • Buoy vs airport (4,530 simultaneous race-window readings): the buoy is 9 % stronger than LEPA at the median (+1.1 kt on average). The gap is largest under northerlies, W–WNW and synoptic S (+1.6 kt), smallest in sea breeze (+0.5 kt) and NE–E (+0.8 kt). Buoy direction is a median 5° anticlockwise of LEPA (sea breeze −9°, northerly −7°).
  • Portals vs the buoy, according to AROME HD only (2,146 winter hours): Portals at 0.74× the buoy-point wind and 3° clockwise of it. Lightest relative to the buoy under NW–N (0.63×) and W (0.70×), closest under NE–E (0.88×). This fits Portals sitting in the lee of the Serra de Tramuntana, but it has not been measured.
Rule of thumb until on-board logs say otherwise: expected Portals wind ≈ buoy × 0.75, or ≈ LEPA × 0.8. Under northerlies expect Portals to be lighter still, and gustier than either station.

8Genoa choice

From North Sails' Dragon Speed Guide: medium genoa 6–18 kt, heavy genoa 14–30 kt. North describes the V-6H as "the 'go to' sail for Dragon teams when the wind is above 15 knots but can be powered up nicely when the wind speed drops".

9Open questions for the on-board logs

What the Vakaros logs from race days should answer:

  1. Is the real Portals / buoy ratio about 0.74, as AROME says? Is it lower under northerlies?
  2. What are the shift period and amplitude on the course? The buoy only samples every 10 min.
  3. Under northerlies, is there a persistent left (backing) bias? Under NE–E, a persistent veer to the right?
  4. When does the Embat actually reach Portals compared with the buoy (lag in minutes)? Does it arrive from the SW side of the course first?
  5. What are the real gust factors near the shore? The buoy says 1.11–1.15.
  6. At what corrected wind speed does the V-6M → V-6H change actually pay?
  7. Is AROME's NE–E error (2 kt too light, about 15° too far clockwise) the same at Portals?
  8. Which side of the course (west or east) gets more wind, and in which regimes?

Caveats

  • Location: the buoy is at 39.499°N 2.702°E, about 12 km ESE of the race area, in open water. The only observations at Portals itself are the Weathercloud station's, archived since 16 Oct 2026 (30 hourly values), too few for statistics. Read every buoy number as "the middle of the bay", not as Portals.
  • Sample gaps: buoy winters are partial. Nov–Feb data cover Dec 2017–Jan 2018, Dec 2018–Jan 2019, early Nov 2019, Jan–Feb 2020, Nov–Dec 2020, Nov 2021, Nov 2022, Nov–Dec 2023 and Nov 2025. No February data after 2020, and none for winter 2024-25.
  • Direction fault: from 17 Nov to 24 Dec 2023 the buoy's direction read 97–99° off the airport and every model, so its direction was masked (speed still used).
  • LEPA is inland at the airport east of Palma, with terrain-steered wind and directions rounded to 10°, which pushes its trends toward 0°. Buoy and airport agree on the regime on only 65 % of 404 shared days.
  • The Portals / buoy ratio (0.74×) is a model estimate from AROME HD, not a measurement.
  • The west vs east course gradient is undetermined: neither station spans the race course.
  • Regime classification uses objective rules on the buoy's 11–16 h mean wind (sea breeze: from 150–250°, under 16 kt, with a morning land breeze or calm; light: under 4 kt; otherwise by sector: N 290–045°, NE–E 045–120°, S–SE–SW 120–250°, W 250–290°). These are not an official classification.
  • Model scores cover Oct–Mar against the buoy's hourly mean; direction scored only when the buoy reads at least 4 kt. They reflect short-range skill.
  • Buoy shift statistics cannot see cycles shorter than about 20 min (10-min sampling). Gusts near the mountains at Portals are likely stronger than the buoy's 1.11–1.15 gust factor.
  • Live-station comparison covers 16 Oct 11:00 – 17 Oct 16:00 Madrid time (30 hourly values) and mostly uses an approximate hourly value for Portals (midpoint of Weathercloud's published hourly min/max).
  • Tactics are inference from statistics and venue notes; check them against your own observations.
