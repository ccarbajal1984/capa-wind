Rig set-up sequence (dock)

Petticrows

  1. Jumpers – fixed avg 8 Loos; adjustable mark rope at 6/8/10.
  2. Mast vertical, fully fwd, mark 81 cm up, mark forestay.
  3. Rake = forestay mark to deck, average 122 (121 heavy, 123 light).
  4. Tape Stn 4 line.
  5. Upper front face 82 cm, lower 85 cm (aft = less bend).
  6. Forestay plate front 104 cm.
  7. Mast plate aft end 82 cm.
  8. J ≤186 cm.
  9. Pre-bend 0–10 mm.
  10. Uppers 15, lowers next hole aft 10, ram lever max aft = mast level with aft of plate.
  11. Set tension for conditions.
  12. Runner: forestay 32 Loos = absolute max, tape the wire.

Source: petticrows_setup_full_2026, via DRAGON_COACH.md §2.

North

Mark wires at black band 80 cm; forestay 186 cm from front of mast; lowers ~3 cm behind uppers; light crews (220-240 kg) shrouds one hole fwd; runner max: rig at 20 V / 8 D, pull runners to forestay 30, tape → eased “just tight” mark ≈15 cm above deck; lowers keep mast straight up to 20-22 kn, then tighten until mast drops 5-10 cm to leeward at hounds.

  1. D 22 at +20 kn vs GC max-tight 12 D, Petticrows 15, Fritz ≤12, North set-up 8. Possibly a typo or a different gauge/scale.

  6. “-35 (PT2)”, (7,487 m spi halyard confirmed by Carlos; turns column = total turns with step from previous row in brackets, confirmed), “X mark at station #10” – meaning unclear; record what each refers to.
  7. Runner (burda) “45-60 %” – percentage of what (scale on control line?) – record the reference.

Gear shifting

  • Runner tension is the gear-shift control: light = less runner → forestay sag → power. Building = more runner + ram max aft so the mast doesn’t over-bend. Windier still = lots of runner + ease ram to bend mast, but keep headstay tension.
  • Waves: twistier set-up, softer sheet, open leech; balance the boat – big keel-hung rudder = drag if over-steered.
  • Barberhauler up = lead aft = sheet harder/flatter; >20 kn second barberhauler pulls lead ~200 mm outboard.
  • Main: boom near centreline, ease traveller as sheet comes on; no vang-sheeting.
  • Genoa first, helm second: in a lift ease genoa, helm comes up slowly, genoa back in.

Source: north_speed_guide.txt, via DRAGON_COACH.md §4.

Crew roles

  • Forward (genoa trimmer/often tactician): to leeward in light air, sheet, halyard, barberhauler, fine-tunes; calls puffs; hikes when breeze comes. Downwind: bow/mast, holds pole at shrouds, calls wind and traffic; comes back to cockpit when breezy.
  • Middle (main): always to windward; mainsheet, runners, ram, jumpers, traveller, tack, outhaul, vang. Puff: ease sheet/drop traveller so helm can bear off. Spinnaker trimmer downwind at front of cockpit, windward.
  • Helm: sits to leeward in light air, windward 10-12 kn+; pumps main downwind when legal.
  • Crew 285 kg max; most sail 3 (~95 kg avg), some 4.

Source: north_speed_guide.txt, via DRAGON_COACH.md §5.

Manoeuvres

  • Tack: #1 error = releasing genoa too early. Ease slightly, release only when it backs against the rig; <6 kn option: furl genoa. Coarse sheet first, then fine-tune; cleat fast when windy. Flat water: steer slowly; waves: turn quickly. Centre tiller as boat straightens and main tacks. Better over-steer than under – genoa must blow outside leeward shroud. Middle: light = traveller to new windward side first; heavy = runners first.
  • Spinnaker set: (cockpit launch) sneak guy out as far as possible before the set.
  • Downwind: furl genoa, release ram fully, ease runners/backstay so rig goes forward; ease adjustable main tack. From ~8 kn “fly” the kite off the short pole: pole squared to shrouds, clews level, guy up to ~6 ft off the pole. Main top batten parallel to boom; vang tighter if rolling, looser to sail lower. Bow down/stern up in light-moderate.
  • Gybe: time it while surfing / rolling to windward. Trim main in a little; as main crosses haul new runner, ease old ASAP; backstay holds only briefly. Light air: both runners off, backstay holds, keep kite rotating. 7-8 kn guy at pole; 8-10+ exit with tack at pole then ease away. Fritz: gybe with runners open and backstay pulled a little; re-tension windward runner after in heavy air.
  • Takedown: into cockpit, always on port side for next set: release stbd sheet, pull around bow, drop halyard, pull fast.
  • Safety: automatic bilge pumps + manual pumps + 2 buckets; hard rolling can broach to windward and swamp; keep hatches in place.

Source: north_speed_guide.txt unless noted, via DRAGON_COACH.md §6.

Starting

  1. Approach with genoa furled under main; unroll when time/distance right; earlier in light air.
  2. Keep flow over rudder; when genoa comes out aim low enough not to backwind.
  3. Windy: sail as high/slow as possible, bow down at the end; don’t over-ease main or bow blows off.
  4. Gold Cup routine (Nano Negrín): backstay on start mark during sequence until luffed on starboard, then add runner.
