Reading the colours

Ranges match Capa's tuning card. Trend = latest reading vs ~60 min earlier. “Veering” = clockwise shift, “Backing” = anticlockwise. Readings older than the station's normal cadence are greyed as stale; missing stations show n/a. Nothing on this page is estimated or filled in.
